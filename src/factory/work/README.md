# The work area

Work items, their journal, the story form, answers, the archive and the feature acceptance. [The guide page](../../../docs/project-guide/work.md) explains it.

- `adapters/` The Markdown reader and writer of a work item file.
- `application/` Reading and writing work items and the acceptance.
- `domain/` The data and rules of a work item, its journal, the story form, answers, decisions, send-backs, labels and the acceptance.
