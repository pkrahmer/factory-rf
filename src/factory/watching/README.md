# The watching area

What shows the factory and what it costs: the board, the watcher, cost records, bills and transcripts. [The guide page](../../../docs/project-guide/watching.md) explains it.

- `adapters/` The JSONL cost records, the factory log, the health file, the machine files and the transcript files.
- `application/` The board, the watcher, the costs, the transcripts and the machine readings.
- `domain/` The data of the board, cost records, bills, the human's waits, transcripts and the machine's readings.
