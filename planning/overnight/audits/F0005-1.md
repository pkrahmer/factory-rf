# Audit of F0005 (stage protocol and cost), round 1

## Summary

- 5 blockers, 14 should-fixes, 13 notes. Form passes: every story has the five sections of `factory/TICKET.md`, its criteria are numbered and fewer than 26 (largest S0007 with 22), the closing pair is in place, every fence is followed by `Expect:`, no heading sits inside a fence and there are no clock times. No draft needs a new dependency or writes outside `src/*` and `tests/*`.
- Blocker 1 (Q3): S0003 still builds the run record (`RunRecord`, `RunRecordStore`, `JsonRunRecordFile`, `Boundaries.run_record`, c4, c13 to c16, its docs criterion and Demo). S0008 and FEATURE also cite it. The human dropped it.
- Blocker 2: the human settled that F0005 resets `crashes` on a forward move (skeleton-answers, cross-check 1 blocker 2; questionnaire, "Settled, not asked"). Yet FEATURE decision 17, S0006 and S0007 c13 keep `crashes` unchanged.
- Blocker 3: S0007 c5 cannot be met. `output_tail` ends with make's own `make: *** [...] Error 1` line, so the last 15 lines of a 20-line failing check are `line 7` to `line 20` plus make's line. Checked with the built `MakeProjectCommands`.
- Blocker 4: S0008's `begin` writes the sidecar before the run. F0004's undo always reverts or deletes it. Every agent entry then ends with "The factory undid changes …: <sidecar>", which breaks S0005 c12, a test S0008's coder may not change.
- Blocker 5: S0001 sends every run with `environment = {}`. F0001 decision 17 requires F0005 to give the request a `PATH` that holds `factory-guard` and a `HOME` (also Q22). With `{}`, real `claude -p` runs find no login and the guard hook cannot start.
- Main should-fixes:
  - `main() -> None` in S0009.
  - The tester's Mode line is lost after a stall.
  - Rule 1 uses the findings from before the run.
  - Form findings are compared by messages that embed line numbers.
  - A backlog story without `## Journal`.
  - S0004's undefined handling of `HostingServiceError`.
  - The "exactly one commit" claims.
  - A weak SI1.4 search.
  - GH4.2's timed-out case.
  - Unguarded DTO limits.
  - Additions to `tests/domain/test_post_texts.py`, which F0003 wrote.
