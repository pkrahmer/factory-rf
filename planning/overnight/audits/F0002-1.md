# Audit of F0002 (work item data), round 1

## Summary

- F0002 has 2 blockers, 9 should-fixes and 11 notes.
- Blocker 1. F0002-S0007's Demo renders a started record and a completed record that share one run id. The story's own duplicate-run rule makes `render` raise, so the Demo's `Expect:` cannot be met. The Interface example and criterion 8 have the same clash with criterion 9.
- Blocker 2. F0002-S0001 still says "Create the two pure domain modules" and defines `WorkItemId`, `Name`, `REMOTE`, `MAIN_BRANCH` and `LABEL_PREFIX` again. F0001 already built all five, and its decision 3 says F0002-S0001 "must not create them again". Cross-check 1, finding 4, reported this and it is still open.
- The main should-fixes are in four places:
  - The story check command (S0008) has two problems. A started story on `main` has no front matter, so the command reports `no front matter` for every one. Its console-script text is stale.
  - The fault messages in S0003 and S0007 are looser in the criteria than in the Interface, and the empty-front-matter case is open.
  - A backlog story with journal entry 1 counts as complete. The opening then writes a second entry 1.
  - S0007's Demo has a clock time. Two criteria need the `type[BaseModel]` construction to pass `mypy --strict`.
- Questionnaire 1 and decision 008 change S0001, S0003, S0008 and FEATURE.md (v1's folders and keys), plus the console-script text. None of them touches a run-record field.
