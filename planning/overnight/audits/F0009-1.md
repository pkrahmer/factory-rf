# Audit F0009-1: feature acceptance, round 1

## Summary

- 34 findings: 10 blockers, 14 should fixes, 10 notes. The form is clean. Each story has five sections and one title. Criteria run 1 to n (24, 25, 25, 19, 25, 19), with `make check` second to last and `docs:` last. Every Demo block has an `Expect:`, and no fence holds a heading line. There is no front matter, no clock time and no story above 26 criteria. No dependency is new, and every write is under `src/*`, `tests/*` or `docs/*`. Each Demo's `Expect:` matches what the Interface's rules produce against the built F0002 code.
- Blocker 1: an acceptance cannot run its stage. F0005's built stage protocol checks the story form before and after every agent run. The report has none of the form's sections, so decision rule 1 turns the reviewer's forward decision into a question every time. Also, no story calls `acceptance_prompt` from `TaskComposer`, and the FEATURE puts the stage protocol out of scope.
- Blocker 2: a merged acceptance is never read again. `in_flight()` skips an acceptance whose report is on the main branch, and F0006-S0001's `RemoteWorkItems` excludes acceptance ids. So no `merged` event is raised, the status stays `merged_unrecorded` forever, and S0006 c4, c8 and c14 cannot pass. How the in-flight snapshots reach the watcher is not in any Interface.
- Blockers 3 and 4 (Q20): S0006 names neither `build_handlers` nor `NOT_YET_HANDLED`. It leaves F0007's `RunGate` holding the story's `merged` and `closed` handlers. It does not name the wiring tests it breaks: F0006-S0007 c9 and F0007-S0010 c10 and c13, as F0008-S0001 left them. It also registers handlers whose constructors raise `ValueError` for any definition without `acceptance_stage`, so every tick of such a project would fail.
- Blockers 5 to 10: the new handlers do not inherit `Handler`. F0005-S0009's search test refuses `entry_text`, `JOURNAL` and `## Journal` in `factory.domain.acceptance`. The closed models have no criteria for their refused sides. S0005 c20 cannot be met once the branch is gone. S0006 c8 expects a `merged` event that F0006-S0002's evaluation does not give. S0003's `write_paths` duplicates the built `WritePathService.at`.
- The should fixes cost a round each. They cover the due ranking that reads `Event.detail`, a doubled `factory/features/` prefix, the board change without a criterion, and duplicates of F0007 and F0005-S0009 code. They also cover the move-then-write order, the close before the approval, the reject side of ST2.2, ST6.3 in the evaluation, the test setup against the fixture's backlog story, and two stories that are too large.
- Questionnaire 1: Q20 changes the FEATURE, S0003, S0004 and S0006. Q3, Q15 and decision 008 change nothing here. Q2, Q6, Q7, Q11, Q12 and Q13 confirm what the drafts say.
