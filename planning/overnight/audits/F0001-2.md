# Audit of F0001-boundaries, round 2

## Summary

- 3 blockers, 6 should-fixes, 10 notes. Narrow round: the rows of `F0001-1-handling.md`, the changed or added criteria and rules, cross-check 1's findings 3, 4, 12 and 13, and S0009's argument list.
- Most fixes hold: A1 to A4, A6 to A11, A13 to A20, A22 to A34 and the pre-check items read correctly in the stories. Form is clean except S0002 (blocker 1): sections, numbering, the two closing criteria, `Expect:` lines, titles against FEATURE's list, no `#` line in a fence, no clock time.
- Blocker 1: in S0002 the `\r` and `\r\n` of the P-S0002-1 fix became real line breaks. Criterion 9 and its rule are garbled, a blank line splits the criteria list, and the answer's condition is lost.
- Blockers 2 and 3 come from the A12 and A13 fix. `GitCli`'s constructor now has six parameters. `pyproject.toml` sets `max-args = 5` for `src`, and S0004 c14 forbids a `noqa` outside `process.py`, so `make check` cannot stay green. A `GitCli` without an identity can no longer commit, but every Demo outside F0001 builds `GitCli(work)` without one, and some of them commit (F0004-S0001's claim, for example).
- The same fix leaves `merge` without an identity undefined (should fix). S0005 c2 says "a `GitCli` method", but `resolve` and `is_ancestor` may read exit status 1 as "no such ref" or "false".
- S0010 does not say which measures a run has when its resumed call reaches the `time_limit` (R24). It also has no case for a resumed budget reply that exits with status 1.
- S0005, S0006 and S0008 have 24, 25 and 26 criteria with several mechanisms each. That is above the binding size answer's 22.
- (d) `--permission-prompts none` is restored consistently: the Interface list, c1, FEATURE decision 13, and S0010's resume reuses the list. Cross-check 3 holds for F0001 (S0005 c10 and c11, decision 7). Findings 4, 12 and 13 leave F0002 and the sheet to act (notes 8 and 10).
