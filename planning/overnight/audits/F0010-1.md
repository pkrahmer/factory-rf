# Audit F0010-1: the machine, round 1

## Summary

- 50 findings: 15 blockers, 23 should fixes, 12 notes. The form is clean. Each story has a title and five sections, the criteria run 1 to n (20, 19, 19, 19, 19, 11, 14, 19, 16), `make check` is second to last and `docs:` last, and every Demo block has an `Expect:`. No fence holds a heading line, there is no front matter or clock time, no story is above 26 criteria, no dependency is new, and every write is under `src/*`, `tests/*`, `image/*`, `template/*`, `claude/*` or `docs/*`.
- The drafts were written on 2026-10-13 before F0003 to F0006 were built, and most blockers are clashes with what was built since. S0005 puts the preflight between the restore and the load, but the built tick runs fetch, load, then restore (F0006 decision 10). S0006 has the tick raise the agent-file stop, but only `PipelineLoader` raises and clears its stop (decisions.md). S0006 c4 also contradicts F0003's built test that leaves `colour` aside.
- The environment: S0008 scrubs in `TaskComposer` to five names without `HOME`. That breaks F0005 decision 23 and three built F0005 tests, and contradicts F0001 decision 17 and F0006-S0007. S0008 c6 and S0009 c3 and c5 demand "exactly `request.environment`", but the built runtime adds `SWITCHES`, and the stand-in's Python adds `LC_CTYPE`.
- Git: S0008's system `credential.helper`, S0003's global identity and `pull.ff`, and S0009's global `core.sharedRepository` never reach `GitCli`, which ignores system and global configuration (F0001 decision 7, Q17). The identity of `GIT_USER_NAME` and `GIT_USER_EMAIL` is not in the settings (Q18, F0001 decision 10).
- Wiring (Q20): S0001 neither specifies nor tests `build_boundaries`, never builds `handler_factory`, keeps no single `Tick`, and leaves `factory-watch` unwired (F0006 decision 13). S0001 c6 fails on the story's own `FACTORY_REPOSITORY_URL`, which contains `REPOS`.
- The closed models lack criteria for their refused sides: `InstanceSettings` (S0001), and `PreflightStamp` with its `CommitId` (S0005).
- Questionnaire 1: Q22 (a probe of the flags, `PATH` and `HOME`) and Q29 (v1's texts in the shipped agents) are not applied, and both are blockers. Q17, Q18, Q20 and Q23 also need edits. Q3, Q15 and decision 008 change nothing beyond one sentence for the operator guide.
- Among the should fixes, the security ones need the human. `/state` is writable by the agent, so it can reach the guard settings and the health file. The project commands run agent-written code as the token's owner. With `FACTORY_AGENT_USER` unset, every agent runs as `factory`. `sudo` applies its own umask, its process group is beyond the factory's kill, and its `use_pty` matters too. All of these are morning observations at best.
