# Audit F0008-1: crash recovery, round 1

## Summary

- 27 findings: 5 blockers, 12 should fixes, 10 notes. The form is clean: five sections, one title, criteria 1 to n (20, 20, 17), `make check` second to last and `docs:` last, an `Expect:` after every Demo block, no heading line inside a fence, no front matter, no story above 26 criteria, no new dependency, every write under `src/*`, `tests/*` or `docs/*`.
- Blocker 1 (Q20): no story registers `crash` or `expired` in `factory.entry.wiring.build_handlers` or takes them out of `NOT_YET_HANDLED`. The rerun's `run` is not tied to F0007's `RunGate`, and the FEATURE's Out of scope still gives the mapping to F0006/F0010. The tests F0006-S0007 and F0007-S0010 wrote for the wiring would break without a leave named in the story.
- Blocker 2 (Q3): the second Demo of every story imports `factory.adapters.run_record_file` and passes `run_record=` to `Boundaries`. Both are gone; run against the built code this gives `ModuleNotFoundError` and then `TypeError`.
- Blockers 3 and 4: S0002 builds two counter resets that other features already build. The forward-move reset belongs to F0005 decision 17 and S0007 c14, while `decide` returns a `StageMove` with no state (S0006 c13) and "rule 7" is a rework, not the forward move. The crash-cap answer belongs to F0007 decision 15 and `unblock`.
- Blocker 5: F0005 decision 8 hands F0008 the path where a boundary error after a claim repeats and reaches the crash cap. F0008 states and tests that path nowhere.
- The should fixes cost a round each. `RecoveryHandler` does not inherit `Handler`. `ask_crash_cap` posts a body that `AskHandler` would post differently. S0001 c13 builds an `Event` that F0006 refuses. The journal calls the remote head the stage-start commit, which is false after a human push. At the cap S0003 can record the pull request twice and fail with `NothingToCommit`. The Interface misspells `stall_note`'s built signature, and the Demos carry a clock time. "Any comment" contradicts F0007's answer rule. The steps re-implement F0007's `WorkItemRecorder`. The test setup leaves the fixture story without a `## Journal` section, and S0002 extends a test file that S0001 wrote.
- The cross-check's findings 2, 5, 6, 12, 16 and 17 are still open in these drafts. Decision 008 and Q15 change nothing here: F0008 recognises no v1 work item.
