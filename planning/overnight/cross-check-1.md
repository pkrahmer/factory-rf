# Cross-feature check, round 1

## Summary

- 11 features, 76 stories. Ids are unique and contiguous in every feature, every file stem matches its folder, every slug starts with a letter, and every `FEATURE.md` Stories list matches its drafts title for title. Every story has the five sections, one title, the log line, an `Expect:` after every Demo block and no `# ` line inside a fence.
- Every story's last criterion starts with `docs:` and names the documents it touches (WE6.3 holds as an observation). The second-to-last is the literal `make check stays green.` in 38 stories; the other 38 write it with backticks (finding 9).
- The plan's dependency table holds per condition (answer 12). No story's criteria need a story with a higher id, except through the production wiring that no story builds (finding 1).
- Traceability: 87 of 96 conditions are traced to a building story (5 of them as a static test plus a morning observation), 1 is observation only (WE6.3), 8 are not tonight.
- 25 findings: 3 blockers, 10 should fix, 12 notes. The blockers are the missing wiring of handlers and boundaries (1), the `crashes` reset that F0008 makes in F0005's tested code (2), and git configuration that `GitCli` ignores while F0010 relies on it for the factory's own pushes (3).
- Improvers who must act on a blocker or should-fix: F0001, F0002, F0003, F0004, F0005, F0006, F0007, F0008, F0009, F0010, and the main session for the interface sheet.
