## A point that reaches past this story

Criterion 11 runs `[sys.executable, "-I", "-m", "factory.entry.leftovers", ...]`. `-I` implies `-E`, so the child ignores `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1` (which the runtime gives the agent). It writes `__pycache__/*.pyc` for every `factory` module it imports into `src/factory/**` of the checkout. Inside `make test` it does so under the recipe's `umask 022`.

I checked this in a throwaway container. As `agent` under `umask 022`, a `python -I` child wrote `drwxr-sr-x agent` folders `src/factory/__pycache__` and `src/factory/domain/__pycache__`. The factory's `git clean --quiet --force --force -d -X` then failed with `Permission denied`, exit 1. Before S0003 this is harmless. After S0003 every tester and coder `make test` leaves such folders, and the removal after the run fails. The feature's point 1 covers this; edit 4 is the cheap guard on this story's side.
