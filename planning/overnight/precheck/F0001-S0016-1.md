## Facts checked

- A negative measure does reach `AgentRunResult` construction today, and nothing catches it. `_Reply.num_turns` is `int | None`, `_Usage`'s four counts are plain `int`, and `total_cost_usd` is `float | None`, all with no bound. `_result` then builds `AgentRunResult(turns=…, tokens=TokenCounts(…), cost_usd=…)`, where `turns` and the token counts are `NonNegativeInt`. Results on the host:
  - `num_turns: -1`: `run` raises `pydantic_core.ValidationError`.
  - `usage.input_tokens: -1` (with `output_tokens`): `ValidationError`.
  - `usage.cache_read_input_tokens: -1` with both input and output counts: `ValidationError`. Without them, `_tokens` returns `None` and nothing is raised.
  - `total_cost_usd: -0.01`: no error. The run returns its end reason with `cost_usd` `Decimal("-0.01")`, because `AgentRunResult.cost_usd` is unbounded.
  
  So criteria 1, 2, 3 and 5 are red today, and 4 and 6 are green (pins).
