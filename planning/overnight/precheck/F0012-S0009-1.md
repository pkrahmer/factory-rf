## Facts checked

- **`free_bytes`.** `shutil.disk_usage(path).free` is `f_bavail * f_frsize` on Linux, the bytes an unprivileged user may write, and raises `FileNotFoundError` for a missing path. Criterion 3 tests both sides.
- **Interface clauses without a criterion** (stage-intake step 3; `CLAUDE.md` Tests on limits):
  - `read_settings` raises `SettingsInvalid(MIN_FREE_SPACE_VARIABLE, "is not a whole number of gigabytes of at least 1")`; criterion 5 checks only that the variable is named, not the fault's text.
