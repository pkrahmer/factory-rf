## Points for the human, with recommended answers

1. The fault in the feature's point 1 (blocking): (a) a new story before this one, in which the runtime opens the agent's files in the checkout for the group after every call; (b) the human's changes to the `Makefile` and S0001's criterion 11; (c) accept the stops. Recommended: (a). See `F0027-feature-1.md`.
2. The restore's order (decision 5): right. Before the switch to `main`, the branch's own nested `.gitignore` still applies (criterion 6, second row). After the switch, the files it hid would be untracked and kept by `-X`, and `reset_to` has already run.
3. What the removal cannot touch: nothing the factory needs lies in the checkout.
   - The instance's state is `/state`, the agent's home `/home/agent` and the factory's `/home/factory`, and a run's temporary folder lies under the system's temporary folder.
   - The factory's own interpreter is `/opt/factory/.venv`. The agents' `PATH` points there (`production_environment`), and so do the guard hook and the confine wrapper.
   - In the checkout, every target of factory-rf's `make check` reaches `.venv` only through `uv run` (`lint`, `types`, `layers`, `sast`, `licenses` after `uv export`, `secrets`, `test`), and so do `duplicates` and `documents`. So no command uses the checkout's `.venv` before `uv run` has built it.
   - The factory's own files in the checkout (work items, `.costs.jsonl` sidecars, `factory/stops.jsonl`) are tracked or untracked-not-ignored, and `-X` keeps both.
