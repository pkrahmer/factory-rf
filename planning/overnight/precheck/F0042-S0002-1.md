## Points for the human

1. **The `sudoers` fallback of the runbook does not cover the adapter's commands.** Section 5's line allows four exact commands (`snapshot`, `destroy`, `rollback -r`, `diff -H`, each on `tank/factory/vm@base`), and the wrapper `zfs-factory` passes every call through `sudo -n`. The adapter runs `diff -FH` (decision 13), two `list` commands (`advance` and `dataset_exists`) and `--version`; under the fallback `sudo` refuses them and every stage would fail at `advance()` with a `sudo:` line as the detail. Options: (a) when the fallback is needed at all, the `sudoers` line names the adapter's seven exact commands (`zfs --version`, `zfs list -H -o name tank/factory/vm`, `zfs list -H -o name -t snapshot tank/factory/vm@base`, `zfs rollback -r tank/factory/vm@base`, `zfs destroy tank/factory/vm@base`, `zfs snapshot tank/factory/vm@base`, `zfs diff -FH tank/factory/vm@base`), and decision 14's "the four `zfs` commands" reads "the adapter's commands"; (b) the wrapper runs `list` and `--version` without `sudo` and the four writing commands through it (logic in the operator's file); (c) nothing now, F0043's story 2 writes what was found. Recommended: (a), a change to `planning/` only, before the VM's prototype runs. The story itself needs no change for it.
2. **The diff reading beyond the four letters** (edits 2 and 3): a path two lines name is listed once as `added`; a field after the form's (`(+1)`, the link count) is ignored; the escapes are bytes read as UTF-8. Options: (a) take the three (recommended: each costs a clause and a line in criterion 3, and each would otherwise be found on the VM); (b) take the first only and learn the other two in F0043's story 4; (c) drop "holds each path once" instead and let the take meet a path twice.
