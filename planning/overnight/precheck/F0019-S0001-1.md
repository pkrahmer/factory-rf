## Checked against the built code and the kernel's interface

- **The constants.**
  - `WRITE_RIGHTS = 0x7FF2` is bit 1 (`WRITE_FILE`) plus bits 4 to 14 (`REMOVE_DIR`, `REMOVE_FILE`, `MAKE_CHAR`, `MAKE_DIR`, `MAKE_REG`, `MAKE_SOCK`, `MAKE_FIFO`, `MAKE_BLOCK`, `MAKE_SYM`, `REFER`, `TRUNCATE`). That is 12 rights, as decision 2 says, and the probe's `WRITE` has the same value.
  - `FILE_RIGHTS = 0x4002` is `WRITE_FILE | TRUNCATE`. Both may be granted on a non-folder; a folder-only right on a file would make `landlock_add_rule` fail with `EINVAL`, which the type checks before the first call rule out.
- **The attributes.**
  - The attribute of 8 bytes (only `handled_access_fs`) is accepted by every ABI: the kernel copies a shorter struct and zero-fills the rest.
  - The probe passed 24 bytes with zeros. Both are fine, and 8 is the safer choice on a 5.19-era kernel, which would refuse unknown non-zero fields.
  - `struct landlock_path_beneath_attr` is packed, `{__u64 allowed_access; __s32 parent_fd}`, 12 bytes. That matches `_pack_ = 1` in the probe.
- **The numbers.** 444 (`landlock_create_ruleset`), 445 (`landlock_add_rule`), 446 (`landlock_restrict_self`); flag `LANDLOCK_CREATE_RULESET_VERSION = 1`; `LANDLOCK_RULE_PATH_BENEATH = 1`; `PR_SET_NO_NEW_PRIVS = 38`. Correct, and the same on x86_64 and aarch64 (the unified syscall table).
- **The rows of criterion 3** against the kernel's rules:
  - The appends and the in-place truncation of F are allowed (`WRITE_FILE`, `TRUNCATE`).
  - The rename of F inside P is refused with `EACCES`: it needs `MAKE_REG` and `REMOVE_FILE` on P, which has no rule. It stays in one folder, so `REFER` plays no part and the error is not `EXDEV`.
  - The delete of F and the link in O are refused with `EACCES`.
  - The read of O is allowed, because reading is not handled.
  - The child shell inherits the domain.
  - The case `restrict_writes([], [])` (criterion 5) is valid: a ruleset with no rule denies every handled access.
  - Stacking (criterion 6) is the kernel's documented behaviour: each layer must allow the write, up to 16 layers.
- **Criterion 4, the refused inputs.** `os.lstat` order: a missing path gives `FileNotFoundError`, whose `strerror` is `No such file or directory`, as the texts expect. A link is caught by `S_ISLNK` before the type check, so a link to a folder in `folders` reads "is a symbolic link", not "is not a folder", as the row expects.
- **Children and coverage.**
  - Every call of `restrict_writes` runs in a child, so pytest-cov 7 counts none of `landlock.py` but `abi_version` and the module's top. The total coverage stays far above 90 %: about 60 uncovered statements against a suite at 99.44 %.
  - `make mutants` will show survivors in `landlock.py`, and decision 18 says so.
- **mypy --strict.** `libc.syscall` returns `Any`, so `warn_return_any` makes the coder wrap it in `int(...)`. That is routine and no stop. `ctypes.get_errno()` and `os.strerror` are typed.
- **Write paths.**
  - Coder: `src/factory/domain/confinement.py`, `src/factory/domain/errors.py`, `src/factory/adapters/landlock.py` (all under `src/*`).
  - Tester: `tests/domain/test_confinement_abi.py`, `tests/adapters/test_landlock.py` (`tests/*`).
  - Documenter: `docs/project-guide.md`, `docs/decisions.md` (`docs/*`). The section *The undo* exists (line 1230), so the new section has its anchor.
- **Budget and time.** About 80 lines of code and about 200 lines of tests in children. Well inside 40 minutes, the tester's $2 and the coder's $4.
