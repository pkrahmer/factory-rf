# Pre-check F0001 (Boundaries), round 3

A narrow re-audit and pre-check in one. I read every row of `audits/F0001-2-handling.md`, every criterion, rule and Demo that round 2 changed, and every predicted refusal of `precheck/F0001-2.md`. I judged them against v1's `stage-intake`, `role-tester`, `role-coder` and `stage-demo`, against `CLAUDE.md`, `pyproject.toml`, `Makefile` and `factory/stages.yml`, and against the round 1 and round 2 answers in `skeleton-answers.md`. S0001 is out of scope.

Facts checked against tools, not taken on trust:

- **`GitOptions` passes ruff and mypy.** I wrote the `GitOptions` model as S0005's Interface gives it (frozen, `extra="forbid"`, `arbitrary_types_allowed`, a `GitIdentity(...)` default and a `{}` default), with `GitCli.__init__(self, directory, options: GitOptions | None = None)`. Pinned ruff 0.17.0 with the project's configuration reports nothing beyond my probe's own import order: no `B008`, `B006` or `PLR0913`, and `RUF` is not selected. `mypy --strict` with the `pydantic.mypy` plugin reports no issue.
- **Pydantic 2 behaves as the criteria need.** Two `GitOptions()` do not share their `environment` dict. A `KillPoints` instance passed in is kept by identity (`options.kill_points is k`), so tests that arm the `kill_points` fixture reach the `checkout` fixture's `GitCli`. An unknown key raises `ValidationError`, and so does an assignment.
- **GNU make 4.4.1 under `MAKELEVEL=1`, `MAKEFLAGS=w` and `MFLAGS=-w`.** Unchanged, it prints `make[1]: Entering directory …`, `ok`, `1` and `Leaving directory …`. With the three variables removed it prints `ok` and `0`, and it does so whether or not `--no-print-directory` is passed.
- **Form.** Checked by script: no control character other than a line feed in any draft, no line with an odd number of backticks, criteria numbered without gaps, and the five sections in every draft.
