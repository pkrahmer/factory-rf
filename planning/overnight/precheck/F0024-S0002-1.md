What only the real factory shows, and what it means for this story: the factory's KILL at a command's time limit goes to the process group of `make`, but `timeout` in the recipe puts mutmut into a process group of its own. So for `mutants` this line appears only when the recipe overran its 10 minutes, and it then names the seconds the factory waited, which can exceed the limit. That is right as a record and does not change the story; the feature's decision 4 should say it (see `F0024-feature-1.md`, point 2).
