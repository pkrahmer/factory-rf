## The test the story leaves out

`tests/adapters/test_claude_runtime_resume.py::test_model_duration_of_a_resumed_run_adds_the_known_calls[resume ended by the time limit]` has a limit of 5 s (`timedelta(seconds=5)`, its fourth row). It asserts `len(_calls(tmp_path)) == 2`.

- Within those 5 s, the first stand-in call must start, print its budget stop and end.
- Then the resume must start and write its own call record.

That is two starts before the limit, the same race as the failed row, and decision 7's test ("the child must do something before the kill") includes it. It passed in all three loaded runs at 5 s, but it is in the modules the smoke test and criterion 6 run. Its resume waits 60 s, above the bound, so `ROOMY_LIMIT` is all it needs (edit 1). The feature's decision 9 and its counts change with it (see `F0029-feature-1.md`).

The kept-short tests of decision 7 check out. The two version rows `exit-1` and `nothing` of `test_a_version_that_cannot_be_read_is_a_runtime_error` (`tests/adapters/test_opencode_runtime_launch.py`, 3 s) would pass but check nothing if their child were killed early, the same kind of row decision 7 counts among the fifteen. The risk is a version line within 3 s; I recommend leaving them (point 3).
