## Points for the human

1. **The owner, as which uid.** The edits take `uid` from the owner of the folder `/proc/<pid>`, which is the process's effective user id. The alternative is the real uid from the `Uid:` line of `/proc/<pid>/status`, but that is a second file and a second parser per process, the same in F0030's kill rounds. `kill(-1)` as `agent` reaches what has `agent` as its real or saved uid. An agent's process cannot change its uid (no privilege; `sudo` gives it none), so for the agent both values are the same. The folder also kept its owner for a process that is not dumpable (prototype). Recommended: the folder's owner, as in the edits.
2. **Where the reader lives.** The edits make F0030's reader public in `factory.adapters.process` and leave `own_identity` and `signal_every_other` in the new `factory.adapters.own_processes`. The other choice is to move both into `factory.adapters.process`, so that the module that kills a session also sends the program's signal. Then `own_processes.py` falls away, and the human's change 1 must name `factory.adapters.process.signal_every_other` instead. Recommended: as in the edits, because it keeps F0030's module change to one renamed function and one sorted return, and the human's change 1 stays as written.
3. **The human's changes 1 and 2 (`CLAUDE.md`) are still to do** before S0001 starts. Their texts stay valid with these edits.
4. **F0040 (project commands as the agent's user):** round 2's point 2 stands unchanged.
