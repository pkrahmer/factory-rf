## Checked against the built code and tests

- **One `Popen` line.** Both functions call a private start helper that holds the line and its markers. This is the coder's design, and the Interface requires it.
- **Reading two pipes from the calling thread.** `selectors` on the two pipes (Linux) or two reader threads feeding a queue both fit the Interface. The callback must run on the calling thread.
  - Merged stderr gives one pipe.
  - The time limit is a deadline in the loop. On a stop, the loop kills the group, drains the pipes until they close, and passes no further line.
- **Criterion 9 needs no sleep.** The child waits for a file that the callback writes. The test needs a time bound so that a failure does not hang. A `time_limit` on the request gives it.
- **Criterion 6.** The child prints its pid first, so `is_gone(pid)` can check it. The tester writes it that way.
- **ruff.** A loop over `selectors` with a deadline, a stop and a kill can pass `C901` 8 if it is split into helpers. That is the coder's design and not a stop.
- **Coverage.** Every branch runs in-process with real children, so nothing is left only to a child, as `confine.py` was in F0019.
- **mutmut.** Mutants of `process.py` start real children. F0019's crash from `mutants/seed/.git` is fixed on `main` by `81fd6131` (the `EXIT` trap).
- **Size.** About 80 lines and 12 criteria: a small story.
