Facts checked with tools, from a scratch directory outside the repository, through `uv run --no-sync --project C:\Dev\Projects\factory-rf`:

- **python-frontmatter 1.3.0 source** (`default_handlers.py` and `__init__.py`), compared with the facts that S0003 states:
  - `DEFAULT_POST_TEMPLATE` is `"{start_delimiter}\n{metadata}\n{end_delimiter}\n\n{content}\n"`.
  - `BaseHandler.format` calls `.strip()` on the whole result.
  - `Post.__init__(content, handler=None, **metadata)` keeps `content` as it is.
  - `YAMLHandler.load(fm, **kwargs) -> Any`.
  - `BaseHandler.export(metadata: dict[str, object], **kwargs: object) -> str`.
  - `detect` is `FM_BOUNDARY.match`, and `split` is `FM_BOUNDARY.split(text, 2)`.
- **A prototype of S0003's reader and writer**, built from the story's rules:
  - It has `RuamelYamlHandler(YAMLHandler)` with two `@override` methods. `load` uses `YAML(typ="safe").load`, with `str(key)` on the keys. `export` uses a round-trip `YAML()` with a `null` representer, a flow `CommentedSeq` and `SingleQuotedScalarString`.
  - The reader calls `detect`, then `split` and then `load` on the text with `\r\n` turned into `\n` and the whitespace around it removed.
  - The writer calls `frontmatter.dumps(frontmatter.Post(body, None, **values), handler=...)`.
  - Stand-ins for S0002's models were used.
- **Results of the probe against the prototype:**
  - c1 gives exactly the example of the Interface, with `started_at: '2026-10-13T08:30:05Z'`. c2, c3, c6, c7, c8, c16 (all five empty forms) and c17 hold.
  - c9 holds in every case: `# T`, `--- x`, `- - -`, two thematic breaks after `# T`, never closed, a closing ` ---`, `stage: [`, a list. A duplicate key gives `front matter is not valid YAML`, because ruamel's `DuplicateKeyError` is a `YAMLError`.
  - c10 holds for all four texts. The c22 identities hold on the class and on an instance.
  - Demo 1 prints exactly fifteen lines, as its `Expect:` says. Demo 2 prints `unknown key: colour` and `None ['stage', 'attempts'] '# T\n'`.
  - c4 holds only when the writer removes the whitespace around the body itself. Passing the body as it is makes `"\n\n# T\n\n\n"` give `---\n\n\n\n# T\n`.
- **Checks of the prototype with the project's configuration:**
  - `ruff check` reports nothing beyond an `assert` in my probe helper, so no `ARG002` on the overrides and no `D102`.
  - `mypy --strict` with the pydantic plugin reports no issue once `YAMLError` is imported from `ruamel.yaml.error`. From `ruamel.yaml` it is `attr-defined`.
  - `bandit` reports nothing beyond the same `assert`.
  - `semgrep` with `security/semgrep` reports nothing. A control file shows that the rule reports `yaml = YAML(typ="safe"); yaml.load(t)` and not `handler.load(t)`.
- **ruamel.yaml safe loading of keys.**
  - `1: x` gives the key `int` 1, `true: y` gives `bool` True, `null: z` gives `None`, and `2026-10-13: d` gives a `date`. So `str(key)` gives `"1"`, `"True"`, `"None"` and `"2026-10-13"`.
  - The key's text and `str(key)` therefore differ for `true` and `null`.
  - `1: x` together with `true: y` is a `DuplicateKeyError`, because `True == 1`.
