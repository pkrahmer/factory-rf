# Pre-check F0020-S0013, round 1: a limit's wait on standard error and a stop on a stream's last line are pinned

I checked the story against v1.1's `claude/agents/intake.md`, `factory/story-form.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and F0020's `feature.md` (decisions 5 and 12) on today's `main`, with `src/factory/adapters/opencode_runtime.py`, `src/factory/adapters/process.py`, `src/factory/domain/provider_limits.py`, the stand-ins and `tests/process_support.py`.

## What I ran

Throwaway containers of `factory-self-factory` (`--network none --cpus 2`), on a copy with LF line endings; the host's pure-Python test packages appended after the image's own `site-packages`. The image's venv has no ruff or mypy, and the host's are Windows builds, so I did not run lint or types.

**Form check.** `python -m factory.entry.check_story` on the story: `ok`, exit 0.

**Code facts.** All true today:

- `_Watch.on_line` (`opencode_runtime.py:363`) calls `self._stop_at_limit(line)` for a standard-error line for which `limit_in_text` is true. `_limit_wait` (`:480`) gives `named_wait(watch.limit_text)` on a `provider_limit` end.
- `_LineReader.read_ready` (`process.py:120`) returns `True` after `_pass_last_line` (`:132-133`).
- `named_wait` reads "retry after 120 seconds" as 120 s and "try again in 7 minutes" as 420 s. `limit_in_text` does not match a line without `428`.
- The helpers exist: `Step` in `tests/adapters/claude_stand_in.py`; `stand_in_runtime(..., stderr_first=...)`, `opencode_request`, `step_start`, `stdout`, `text`, `result_text`, `step_finish`, `RESUME_REPLY`, `WAIT_SECONDS` (30) and `BOUND_SECONDS` (20) in `opencode_stand_in.py`; `python_argv`, `Recorder` and `is_gone` in `tests/process_support.py`.
- Neither new module exists yet.
- Docs: `docs/project-guide/agents.md:132` already says that the `limit_wait` is `named_wait` "of the message or line". `processes-and-checks.md:71-72` says that a last line is passed when its stream ends, and that a stop kills at once, but not that the two go together.

**Prototype.** I wrote both test modules as criteria 1 and 2 describe:

- criterion 1: 4 rows;
- criterion 2: 3 rows. The child writes its pid to a file, as `test_process_streaming.py` does. It writes and flushes the last line, closes the stream with `os.close`, and then sleeps, or for `no-stop` exits at once. The `no-stop` row's child exits 0 and writes nothing on standard error, whether it calls `os._exit(0)` or ends normally.

`7 passed in 0.84s`. With both survivors applied by hand:

- `_stop_at_limit(None)`: `[retry-after]` and `[in-minutes]` fail.
- `return False`: `[stdout]` and `[stderr]` fail (30 s, not under 10).

`tests/adapters/test_opencode_provider_limit.py`, `test_process_streaming.py` and `test_process_streaming_reads.py` all pass under both mutants. So the new tests kill exactly the two survivors (`4 failed, 36 passed`).
