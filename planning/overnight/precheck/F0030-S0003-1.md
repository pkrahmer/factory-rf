**The new fact: the answer to F0030's decision 14.** In the human's real check today, the factory killed `make mutants` at 50 s, during mutmut's stats run. The kill ended all of `make`'s session. But a fake `claude` that `tests/adapters/test_claude_runtime.py::test_call_over_its_time_limit_is_killed_with_unknown_measures` (line 374) started outlived the kill for up to its 60 s, in a session of its own. The reason is in the code under test: `run_process` starts every command with `start_new_session=True` (`src/factory/adapters/process.py:104`). So every process that any test starts through `factory.adapters.process` is outside `make`'s session. When pytest is killed, nothing ends it, and it runs until it ends by itself. I surveyed the waits of the tests' fakes (`wait_seconds=`, `time.sleep(...)`, `sleep N`): the longest is 60 s. So the leftover is bounded today.

This changes the story in two places:

- **The docs criterion is wrong as written.** It says "mutmut and what it started are gone". What mutmut's tests started through the process module is not gone. The criterion must name the exception, or the documenter writes a sentence the real check contradicts (edits 2 and 3).
- **The decision log has no answer to decision 14.** F0030's entry already puts `setsid` outside the rule. One sentence there says that mutmut's tests do start such processes, and why (edit 2).

**The criterion "nothing changes".** Every one of the fifteen documents-only stories in `done/` (F0018-S0001 to F0031-S0003) has criterion 1 "Nothing under `src/` or `tests/` changes. The tester and the coder each end with an entry saying so." This story has no such criterion. Without it, a tester could write a test that greps the documents. That test would be red until the documenter runs, and the coder could not make it green. Edit 1 adds the criterion.

**The pointer's place.** The criterion puts the parenthesis "at its end" of decision (4). There it would follow a full stop and start with a small letter. The entry at line 503 puts its pointer inside the sentence. Edit 2 puts it after "does not reach mutmut".

**Scope.** Documents only, in the documenter's write paths. There are no earlier tests, so no leave is needed.

**Clashes.** No open story names *The temporary folder*, *Mutation testing per story* or F0030's entry.

## Points for the human

1. **Decision 14 is answered: a test that mutmut runs leaves the session.** It is not mutmut itself. It is the factory's own process module under test, which starts every command in a session of its own. Options:
   - (a) Document it and stop there (edit 2). The process ends by itself within the fakes' longest wait (60 s today). It runs only in a sweep, which the human starts by hand in a throwaway copy.
   - (b) A later code story that ends such processes, for example by tracking the sessions that the tests' children start. That brings back the walk of parent ids that F0030's decision 3 rejected, or needs a cgroup that rootless Podman does not give.

   Recommended: (a). The cost is at most a minute of a leftover sleeping fake in a throwaway copy, and (b) adds a fragile rule for that.
2. **The removal by hand.** The trap runs `chmod -R u+rwX` before `rm -rf`, because a stopped pytest can leave folders without write permission. Edit 4 (optional) gives the operator that same command.
